Allama Muhammad Iqbal

Introduction

Allama Muhammad Iqbal was a famous poet, philosopher, and thinker of the Indian subcontinent. He wrote poetry in Urdu and Persian and inspired Muslims through his ideas about self-respect, education, and unity.

Famous Works

  • Bang-e-Dra
  • Bal-e-Jibril
  • Asrar-e-Khudi

His Literary Contributions

Iqbal's poetry encourages individuals to develop their potential and work for a better society.
